# Founder Fieldwork

## Learning architecture

The reader rejected the original multi-column information architecture and the subsequent addition of interactive panels.

- Organize around the learner's next decision, not around installed skills or component coverage.
- Use one reading lane and one active phase: Learn, Check, or Apply.
- Keep course navigation, reference material, and optional demonstrations out of the lesson's main teaching path.
- A visual earns its place by clarifying a difficult distinction. Do not add one to every lesson for consistency.
- Never generate a project note on the reader's behalf or equate a non-empty template with learning.
- Preserve the established brand, existing local notes, source boundaries, and recovery behavior.
- Verify interface improvements separately from learning gains. Do not claim comprehension or retention improvements without learner evidence.
