What came from the reel, and what was added?

The public reel by @davecto says building an app and building a business are different jobs. Its caption names Paul Graham, Y Combinator, and The Mom Test, but offers the full nine-resource list through a comment-triggered message.

That full list was not available in the inspected public material. This is an independently curated companion, not a reconstruction of the creator's list. We selected readings from the named sources, then added Stripe, Rahul Vohra's Superhuman case study, and Sam Altman's playbook. Each lesson labels its provenance.

The receipt-chasing business is a fictional running example. Practice counts measure course activity, never business viability. No market-size claims, startup-success probabilities, or universal product-market-fit thresholds are implied.

    Public source pages and titles checked on 18 October 2026. Advice is context-dependent. Test it against your customers, constraints, and responsibilities.
